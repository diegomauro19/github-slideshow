# Statement of Work (SOW) Template

The SOW is the binding scoping document for an engagement. It sits under a Master Service Agreement (MSA) and references the legal terms of that MSA. The SOW is shorter and more operational than the proposal — it captures only what is being committed to.

> **Length target**: 8–15 pages. If your SOW is longer than 15 pages, you're either putting MSA-class terms into the SOW, or you haven't scoped tightly enough.

---

## 1. Parties and Reference

```
Statement of Work No. [SOW-NNNN]
Under Master Service Agreement dated [MSA Date]

Between:
[Client Legal Entity], with offices at [address] ("Client")
And:
[Agency Legal Entity], with offices at [address] ("Agency")

Effective Date: [date]
```

---

## 2. Engagement Summary

One-paragraph narrative description of what's being engaged. The "this is what we're doing" sentence in plain language.

Example:
> Agency will conduct an AI Readiness Audit for Client's Wholesale Banking division, producing a current-state assessment, prioritized opportunity portfolio, and 18-month roadmap. Engagement runs 10 weeks from the Effective Date.

---

## 3. Scope of Work

### 3.1 In Scope

Numbered list of activities and deliverables. Reference the deliverables catalog by ID.

Example:
1. Stakeholder interviews — up to 12 interviews of 45 min each
2. Document review — up to 5 strategy documents and 3 architecture documents
3. AI Opportunity Map (Deliverable D2.1)
4. AI Readiness Assessment (Deliverable D2.2)
5. 18-Month Roadmap (Deliverable D2.4)
6. Executive readout (90 minutes, on-site or remote at Client's option)

### 3.2 Out of Scope

Explicit list of things not included. Pre-empts the "we assumed you would also..." conversation.

Example:
1. Implementation of any system or workflow
2. Selection or procurement of any vendor or platform
3. Negotiation with third-party vendors on Client's behalf
4. Training of Client's broader workforce (limited training of program team is in scope)
5. Ongoing support after the final readout

---

## 4. Deliverables and Acceptance

Per-deliverable table:

| Deliverable | Format | Due (week) | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery Report (D1.1) | PDF | Week 3 | Contains stakeholder map, current-state summary, and themes; reviewed by exec sponsor |
| AI Opportunity Map (D2.1) | PDF + spreadsheet | Week 7 | Contains scored use case longlist and detailed write-up of top 5 |
| AI Readiness Assessment (D2.2) | PDF | Week 7 | Scored across 5 readiness dimensions with evidence per score |
| 18-Month Roadmap (D2.4) | PDF | Week 9 | Wave plan with use cases, dependencies, and investment estimates |
| Executive Readout | Live session + recorded | Week 10 | 90 minutes, attended by exec sponsor and at least 3 steering committee members |

**Acceptance process**: Client has 5 business days from delivery to provide written acceptance or detailed feedback. If neither is provided in that window, the deliverable is deemed accepted. Detailed feedback triggers one revision cycle within scope.

---

## 5. Engagement Team

| Name | Role | Allocation | Accountable for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Partner Name] | Engagement Partner | 15% | Quality, client relationship, escalation |
| [Principal Name] | Engagement Lead | 80% | Day-to-day delivery, all deliverables |
| [Senior Name] | Senior Consultant | 100% | Analysis, drafting, workshops |
| [Specialist Name] | Governance Specialist | 20% | Governance recommendations |

**Substitution**: Agency may substitute team members only with Client's prior written approval, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Substitutes will be of equivalent or greater seniority.

---

## 6. Client Responsibilities

What we need from the client to do our work. Critical because most engagement delays are client-side, and a clear list creates accountability.

Example:
1. Designate a single point of contact within 5 business days of the Effective Date
2. Provide access to identified stakeholders for interviews within 2 weeks of request
3. Provide requested documents within 5 business days of request
4. Approve deliverables or provide feedback per Section 4 within 5 business days
5. Make available a Steering Committee for two checkpoints
6. Ensure interview participants have authority and information to discuss the topics

**Delay clause**: If Client delays beyond the timeframes above, Agency may extend the schedule day-for-day and/or invoke change control.

## 7. Schedule and Milestones

Visual timeline (Gantt-style) plus milestone table:

| Milestone | Target Week |
|---|---|
| Engagement kickoff | Week 1 |
| Stakeholder interviews complete | Week 4 |
| Discovery readout | Week 5 |
| Roadmap draft | Week 8 |
| Final executive readout | Week 10 |
| Engagement close | Week 11 |

## 8. Investment

```
Total fixed fee:                 $XXX,XXX

Payment schedule:
  50% on Effective Date:         $XXX,XXX  (invoice on signature)
  50% on Final Readout:          $XXX,XXX  (invoice on Week 10)

Plus expenses:
  Travel (if any):               at cost, with Client pre-approval for any single expense > $500
  Out-of-pocket:                 included up to $5,000; above this, with pre-approval

Payment terms: Net 30 from invoice date.
Late payment: 1.5% per month after net terms.
```

For T&M-based SOWs, replace fixed fee section with:

```
Time and materials, capped at $XXX,XXX

Day rates:
  Partner:                  $X,XXX
  Principal:                $X,XXX
  Senior Consultant:        $X,XXX
  Consultant:               $X,XXX

Estimated days: [XX]
Maximum days without change order: [XX]

Billed monthly in arrears against actual hours.
```

---

## 9. Change Control

Any change to scope, deliverables, schedule, team, or fees follows this process:

1. Either party submits a written change request describing the change
2. Agency provides an impact analysis within 5 business days (effort, schedule, cost)
3. Both parties sign a Change Order before work on the change begins
4. Approved Change Orders become amendments to this SOW

**No verbal change requests are honored.** Casual "hey can you also..." asks must be re-scoped through this process or declined.

---

## 10. Specific Terms

Anything in this engagement that deviates from or supplements the MSA. Examples:

- **Reference rights**: Agency may name Client publicly as a customer 90 days after engagement start. Public case study requires separate written approval.
- **Data handling**: Specific provisions if dealing with regulated data
- **Travel**: Specific provisions on locations, frequency, expense caps
- **Working hours**: Standard 9-5 in Client's time zone unless otherwise specified
- **Intellectual Property**: Standard MSA terms apply — agency methodology remains agency IP; client-specific deliverables and code created during engagement become Client IP upon final payment

## SOW review checklist

Before sending an SOW for signature:

- [ ] References the correct MSA by date and name
- [ ] All deliverables in Section 4 have unambiguous acceptance criteria
- [ ] Client responsibilities in Section 6 are specific and time-bound
- [ ] Investment matches the proposal exactly (or change is documented)
- [ ] Out of Scope list explicitly addresses anything that came up in discussion
- [ ] Payment schedule aligns with our standard policy
- [ ] No material differences from the proposal (if any, explicitly flagged)
- [ ] Reviewed by partner-in-charge
- [ ] Reviewed by legal if any non-standard terms
- [ ] Sent with cover email summarizing what changed from any prior version
