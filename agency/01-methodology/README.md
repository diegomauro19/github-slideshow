# Methodology

Our methodology is how we deliver value across every engagement — from the first audit to a five-year managed-services partnership. It's the spine of the agency: it makes our work repeatable, our quality predictable, and our pricing defensible.

## What we believe

1. **AI is a leverage tool, not a strategy.** Every engagement starts with the client's business outcome. AI is the means; the outcome is the deliverable.
2. **Production beats prototypes.** Most enterprise AI dies between POC and production. Our work is structured to cross that gap by default — every artifact targets the production system, not the demo.
3. **Vendor-neutral by default.** We don't resell a model, framework, or platform. We pick the right tools for each problem and document why.
4. **Measurement is non-negotiable.** No engagement closes without a baseline, a target, and an instrumented system to track both.
5. **Hand off, don't hand over.** The client team must be able to run, modify, and extend what we build. Documentation, training, and runbooks are deliverables, not afterthoughts.

## The five phases

```
1. DISCOVER  →  2. ASSESS  →  3. DESIGN  →  4. BUILD  →  5. SCALE
   (2 weeks)     (3-4 weeks)   (2-3 weeks)   (8-16 weeks)  (ongoing)
```

| Phase | Goal | Decision gate at end |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | Understand the business, the people, the data, the constraints | Go/no-go on engagement scope |
| 2. Assess | Quantify the opportunity and the readiness | Approve roadmap and budget |
| 3. Design | Choose the architecture, model, integrations, success metrics | Approve build plan |
| 4. Build | Ship a production-grade system with monitoring, evals, runbooks | Production cutover |
| 5. Scale | Operate, iterate, and expand | Renewal or graduation |

Each phase has fixed deliverables, a defined client checkpoint, and a clear exit criterion. The phases are sequential at the engagement level but can run in parallel across multiple use cases within a larger program.

## When to skip phases

Not every engagement runs all five. Common variations:

- **Strategy-only**: Discover → Assess → Design. Output: a roadmap. Build is left to the client or future engagement.
- **Build-only** (after a prior strategy engagement): Design → Build → Scale. Skips re-discovery if the strategy phase is < 6 months old.
- **Audit**: Discover → Assess only. Output: an opportunities-and-risks report. Used as a foot-in-the-door offer.
- **Managed services**: Scale only. Used when we inherit a system the client (or another vendor) built, and we operate it.

See `phases.md` for the detailed playbook for each phase, and `frameworks.md` for the analytical frameworks we apply within them.

## Why this works

- **Predictability for the client** — they always know what comes next, what they'll receive, and what decision they'll be asked to make.
- **Predictability for us** — every team member knows the playbook. Quality stops depending on individual judgment.
- **Risk control** — the gate between Assess and Design lets us walk away from a bad fit before either party is committed to a build.
- **Composability** — phases plug together cleanly. We can sell any subset and clients can re-engage at any phase.
