# Org & Processes

How the agency operates internally — roles, responsibilities, decision rights, recurring meetings, and the tooling stack. This section is for partners, hiring managers, and anyone joining the firm.

## Operating principles for the agency itself

The same principles that govern client engagements govern how we run the firm:

1. **Senior bias internally too.** A small senior team beats a big mixed team. Every hire is for someone who could lead an engagement within 12 months.
2. **Documented over tribal.** Every recurring decision becomes a written principle in this repo. Knowledge that lives only in someone's head is a single point of failure.
3. **Bias to action over coordination.** Default to "do it and tell the team" rather than "ask permission." The constraint is judgment, not headcount.
4. **Profitable from project one.** No vanity revenue. No work below margin to "land the logo." Margin discipline is the difference between a firm and a hobby.
5. **Measure ourselves the way we measure clients.** Quarterly business reviews of our own metrics, internal red-team of our own work, post-mortems on our own losses.

## Org structure for a 3–10 person agency

The starting structure assumes 3–10 people. Beyond 10, additional layers appear (practice leads, regional partners, dedicated ops). At 10 people you should be reading [Patty McCord's *Powerful*](https://pattymccord.com/book/) and [Ben Horowitz's *The Hard Thing About Hard Things*](https://www.benhorowitz.com/the-hard-thing-about-hard-things) to plan the next layer — but don't pre-build it.

```
                       Founding Partner(s)
                              │
                              │
    ┌─────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┐
    │                         │                         │
Engagement Delivery     Business Development        Operations
                                                  (later, when
                                                   needed)
    │                         │                         │
    ├─ Engagement Lead        ├─ Sales partner          ├─ Ops Manager
    ├─ Engagement Lead        │  (often founder         │   (1 FTE @ ~10 ppl)
    ├─ Engineers              │   initially)            ├─ Finance / contracts
    ├─ Engineers              ├─ Marketing              │   (fractional 0-10 ppl)
    └─ Specialists            └─ Partnerships           └─ People / recruiting
       (gov, security, data)                                (fractional 0-15 ppl)
```

## Decision rights at a glance

| Decision | Who decides | Who's consulted |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement scope and price | Engagement Partner | Engagement Lead |
| Discount > 5% | Founding Partner(s) | Engagement Partner |
| Hiring (full-time) | Founding Partner(s) | Hiring manager + 2 panel members |
| Hiring (contractor) | Engagement Partner | Engagement Lead |
| Methodology change | Founding Partner(s) | All Engagement Leads |
| New service / package | Founding Partner(s) | All Engagement Partners |
| Walk away from a deal | Engagement Partner | Founding Partner(s) (if > $250k ACV) |
| Termination of an engagement | Founding Partner(s) | Engagement Partner + legal |
| Personnel performance issue | Manager + Founding Partner | HR / external |
