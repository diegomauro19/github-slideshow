# Commercial Model

How we price, propose, and contract enterprise AI consulting work. This section is for sales, partners, and engagement leads — anyone who quotes or signs.

## Our commercial posture

Three core decisions define our commercial model:

### 1. Senior-only delivery, priced near Big 4 senior-manager rates
Big 4 firms charge partner rates and deliver with 2–5 year associates. We charge senior-manager rates ($2,500–$4,500/day) and deliver with people who have actually shipped production AI systems. Total project cost ends up materially lower because teams are smaller, but day rates are not the discount. **The pitch is value density, not cheapness.** This positioning is consistent with industry analysis showing AI-native premium boutiques (Distyl, forward-deployed shops) commanding rate parity or 10–25% premium on senior talent.

### 2. Pricing model varies by phase
| Phase | Default pricing model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | **Fixed-fee** | Scope is bounded; client wants predictability |
| Assess | **Fixed-fee** | Same |
| Design | **Fixed-fee per use case** | Scope known after Assess |
| Build | **Fixed-fee with change-control**, or T&M with cap | Scope mostly known but reality bites; cap protects client |
| Scale | **Monthly retainer + metered usage** | Reflects actual operating cost, scales with the system |

**Outcome-based fees** (10–25% upside on attributable value) layer on top of the base structure when (a) the outcome metric is unambiguous and instrumented, and (b) the attribution to our work is defensible. They are an accelerator, not the primary revenue model.

### 3. Vendor-neutral pricing
We don't take reseller margin or platform commissions. Our quote is for our work. The client buys infrastructure, models, and tools at their own contracts. This shows up as a credibility advantage in every Big 4 comparison — and it's defensible because we have no incentive to recommend a specific provider.

## Sales cycle

For an enterprise client (target segment), expect:

```
Initial contact → Discovery call (30 min)
                ↓
              First meeting (60-90 min, exec sponsor + 1-2 others)
                ↓
              Scoping session (working session, 2-3 hours)
                ↓
              Proposal (1-2 weeks to write, 2-4 weeks for client to evaluate)
                ↓
              Procurement / legal (4-12 weeks for new vendor; 1-2 weeks if MSA exists)
                ↓
              SOW signed → engagement starts
```

**Total cycle**: 6–16 weeks from initial contact for net-new logos with no MSA. 4–8 weeks for follow-on work with existing clients.

## Win criteria — what makes a deal closeable

Not every prospect is a fit. Use these criteria to qualify:

| Criterion | Must have | Disqualify if |
|---|---|---|
| **Executive sponsor** | Named, accessible, has budget authority | "We need to socialize this with leadership" |
| **Use case clarity** | At least one concrete use case in mind | "We just want to figure out what AI can do" without budget |
| **Budget signal** | Some range mentioned ($X-$Y) | "What does it cost?" with no range and no flexibility |
| **Decision timeline** | <6 months from contact to start | "Maybe sometime next year" |
| **Data accessibility** | Owns the data, or has clear path to access | Critical data locked behind another vendor we can't reach |
| **AI maturity floor** | Has at least basic cloud, identity, and security in place | Greenfield IT — we'd be doing IT modernization, not AI |

Disqualify early. The cost of pursuing the wrong deal is higher than the cost of saying no.

## Anti-patterns we avoid

- **Free POCs to win the deal** — sets the wrong precedent and devalues the work. We do paid Discovery as the foot-in-the-door.
- **Deck-and-pray strategy work** — we don't sell engagements that explicitly stop at "here's a 60-slide strategy deck." Either we go to production or we don't engage.
- **Lowest-price competition** — if a prospect is bottom-shopping for AI services, we exit. We compete on outcomes, speed, and seniority, not price.
- **Resold platforms** — we don't price-bundle a vendor platform into our quote. Client buys directly.
