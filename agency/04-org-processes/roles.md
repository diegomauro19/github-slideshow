# Roles

Every role in the agency, with a clear definition of accountabilities, hiring profile, growth path, and when in the firm's evolution to add the role.

> **Naming convention**: We use "Consultant" for advisory-leaning roles and "Engineer" for build-leaning roles. Some people are both (rare and prized — they earn an "Engineering Lead" title at Principal level).

---

## Founding Partner

**When**: Day 1.

**Accountable for**:
- Firm strategy, brand, and positioning
- Major hiring decisions
- Client relationships at the most senior level (CXO)
- Quality of the methodology
- Profitability and runway

**Profile**:
- Has run an enterprise consulting engagement end-to-end before, ideally multiple
- Has a network that produces 3+ qualified leads per quarter
- Comfortable selling 7-figure engagements
- Comfortable making payroll decisions when revenue is uncertain

**Allocation**: 30% sales, 30% delivery oversight, 20% firm operations, 20% strategy / IP development. Should not be doing day-to-day delivery beyond ~15% of any single engagement.

**Compensation**: Equity-heavy in early years, salary normalizes as firm matures.

---

## Engagement Partner

**When**: Hire #2 or #3 if not a co-founder.

**Accountable for**:
- 2–4 concurrent engagements at the partner level
- Engagement quality and client relationship for each
- Engagement Lead supervision
- Pre-sales support to BD
- Practice contributions (case studies, methodology improvements)

**Profile**:
- 10+ years consulting or technology delivery experience, with at least 3 years in AI/ML
- Has run multiple enterprise engagements as engagement lead before
- Strong written and verbal communication
- Comfortable challenging senior client stakeholders

**Allocation**: 60% delivery (across 2–4 engagements), 20% pre-sales, 20% practice / firm

**Hiring signal to add**: founders are spending >40% of time on direct delivery. Adding a partner buys back founder time.

---

## Engagement Lead (Principal Consultant or Engineering Lead)

**When**: Hire #2–#5.

**Accountable for**:
- One engagement at a time as the on-the-ground lead
- All deliverables of that engagement
- Engagement team (2–4 people)
- Day-to-day client interaction
- Engagement health (schedule, budget, quality, team morale)
- Weekly status report to Engagement Partner

**Profile (Principal Consultant)**:
- 7+ years consulting experience, primarily enterprise AI / data
- Has owned a deliverable end-to-end at multiple Big 4 / boutique engagements
- Expert in our methodology phases 1–3
- Strong workshop facilitation, executive presence

**Profile (Engineering Lead)**:
- 7+ years engineering experience, with 3+ years shipping production AI/ML systems
- Has led a build engagement of >$250k from design through go-live
- Deep technical credibility — clients trust their architecture decisions
- Comfortable in front of CIOs and CTOs

**Allocation**: 80–100% on a single engagement at a time (minor overlap during transitions only)

**Hiring signal to add**: An Engagement Partner is doing day-to-day delivery work because there's no Lead to hand it to.

---

## Senior Consultant / Senior Engineer

**When**: Hire #3–#7.

**Accountable for**:
- Major analysis components or system components within an engagement
- Workshop preparation and facilitation
- Document drafting and quality
- Mentoring more junior staff (when there are any)
- Eval design and execution (engineers)
- Stakeholder interviews

**Profile (Senior Consultant)**:
- 4–7 years consulting or related experience
- Strong analytical chops, structured thinker
- Can write a deliverable with light review

**Profile (Senior Engineer)**:
- 5+ years engineering experience, 2+ in AI/ML production work
- Comfortable with foundation models, RAG patterns, agent frameworks
- Solid software engineering fundamentals (testing, observability, IaC)
- Can run an eval program

**Allocation**: 80–100% billable across 1–2 engagements

---

## Consultant / Engineer

**When**: Hire #5+ as needed.

**Accountable for**:
- Defined work streams within an engagement, supervised by Senior or Lead
- Research, analysis, drafting under supervision
- Code components, eval cases, dashboards (engineers)
- Document preparation, formatting, quality checks

**Profile**:
- 1–4 years experience
- Strong fundamentals, fast learner
- Hungry to grow into Senior role within 18–24 months

**Allocation**: 90% billable

**Hiring philosophy**: We are extremely careful about hiring at this level. The temptation is to add juniors for margin — but our brand is "no learning on the client's dime." Every junior must be assignable to engagements where their work is meaningfully reviewed and their cost defensible. Hire only when the senior team is genuinely overloaded, not as a margin lever.

## Specialist Roles

These are typically narrower-and-deeper hires. They may be on staff (when demand is constant) or fractional (early stage).

### Governance / Risk / Compliance Specialist
**Accountable for**: Governance recommendations, EU AI Act readiness, model risk frameworks, sector-specific compliance (HIPAA, GLBA, FedRAMP).
**Profile**: Lawyer + AI literacy, OR risk professional + AI/ML background. Often a former Big 4 risk practitioner.
**Allocation**: 60% billable across multiple engagements (low intensity per engagement)
**Hire**: As contractor first (~$3k–$4k/day); convert to FTE when utilization > 60%.

### Security / Architecture Specialist
**Accountable for**: Security reviews, threat models, secure architecture patterns
**Profile**: Security architect background with cloud + AI experience
**Allocation**: 50% billable across multiple engagements
**Hire**: Contractor first, convert when utilization > 50%.

### Data Engineering Specialist
**Accountable for**: Data assessments, pipeline design, vector DB / RAG infrastructure
**Profile**: 5+ years data engineering, comfortable with modern stack (dbt, Snowflake/BigQuery/etc., orchestration)
**Allocation**: 70% billable
**Hire**: When 50% of engagements have non-trivial data engineering scope (typically by ~5–7 people).

### MLOps / Platform Engineer
**Accountable for**: Production deployment, monitoring, eval infrastructure, CI/CD for AI
**Profile**: SRE / platform background plus ML systems experience
**Allocation**: 75% billable
**Hire**: When we have 2+ concurrent build engagements requiring production deployment.

## Operations and Support Roles

These support the firm; they are not billable.

### Operations Manager
**When**: ~10 people.
**Accountable for**: Internal tooling, finance ops, contracts admin, vendor management, internal scheduling.
**Profile**: Detail-oriented operator; comfortable wearing many hats.

### Director of Sales / Head of Growth
**When**: ~15 people, when founder sales is no longer enough.
**Accountable for**: Pipeline development, sales process, partnership development.
**Profile**: Has sold enterprise services at $500k+ ACV before.

### Marketing Lead
**When**: ~10–12 people, when content needs to scale.
**Accountable for**: Content (case studies, articles, talks), positioning, brand, conferences.
**Profile**: Technical comfort, opinionated writer.

### People / Recruiting Lead
**When**: ~15 people OR when hiring 5+ people in a year.
**Accountable for**: Recruiting, onboarding, performance management, comp benchmarking.
**Profile**: Has built a team from <10 to >30 before.

### Finance / Contracts (Fractional)
**When**: Day 1 (as fractional / outsourced).
**Accountable for**: Bookkeeping, invoicing, payroll, MSA / SOW review, IP filings.
**Profile**: Lawyer for contracts; CPA for finance. Engage as needed.
**Convert to FTE**: ~20–25 people, or when external spend > $200k/year.

## Career paths

```
Consultant ──→ Senior Consultant ──→ Principal ──→ Engagement Partner ──→ Founding Partner
   (1-3y)          (3-5y)            (5-8y)         (8-15y)               (track)

Engineer ──→ Senior Engineer ──→ Engineering Lead ──→ Engagement Partner
  (1-3y)        (3-5y)            (5-8y)             (8-15y)

Specialist tracks: deepen rather than broaden. Senior Specialist → Principal Specialist → Practice Lead.
```

**Promotion criteria** (for any level):
1. Demonstrated mastery of the current level's accountabilities for 12+ months
2. Has been doing the next level's work for 6+ months (we promote into demonstrated reality, not into stretch goals)
3. Sponsorship from at least 2 partners
4. No active concerns from past clients or teammates

**No "up or out."** We are not Big 4. People who plateau at a level and continue to do excellent work are valued, paid well, and stay.

## Hiring sequence for a new agency

If starting from 2 founding partners, recommended hire sequence:

| Hire # | Role | Triggered by |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Engineering Lead | First build engagement signed |
| 4 | Senior Engineer | First build engagement requires >2 engineers |
| 5 | Engagement Partner OR Principal Consultant | Founders >50% on delivery and dropping balls on sales |
| 6 | Senior Consultant | Strategy demand exceeds founder + lead capacity |
| 7 | Senior Engineer | Second concurrent build |
| 8 | Operations Manager (FTE) OR Senior Engineer | Founder time spent on ops > 1 day/week |
| 9 | Governance Specialist (FTE) | Win rate suffering on enterprise deals because of compliance gaps |
| 10 | Engagement Partner | Founders cannot personally cover all active clients at partner level |

This is a guide, not a rule. Hire to relieve specific pain, not to look bigger.
