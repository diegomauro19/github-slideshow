# Internal Workflows

The recurring processes that keep engagements and the firm running. Each workflow has owners, frequency, inputs, and outputs.

---

## Sales workflows

### W1.1 — Inbound Lead Triage
**Frequency**: Within 24 hours of any inbound  
**Owner**: BD lead (or rotating partner if no dedicated BD)

1. Log lead in CRM with source, contact details, initial ask
2. Apply qualification filter (see [Win Criteria](../02-commercial/README.md#win-criteria--what-makes-a-deal-closeable))
3. If qualified → schedule discovery call within 5 business days
4. If unqualified → polite decline with referral if possible
5. If unclear → 30-min call, then re-triage

**Output**: Logged lead with status (qualified / unqualified / nurture)

---

### W1.2 — Discovery Call
**Frequency**: Per qualified inbound or proactively-sourced lead  
**Owner**: Engagement Partner (always — never delegate first call to non-partner)

**Pre-call** (15 min):
- Read everything about the prospect publicly available
- Check for mutual connections / warm intros
- Check if they're current / prior client of any major firm

**During call** (30–45 min):
- Listen 80%, talk 20%
- Capture: stated problem, exec sponsor, budget signal, timeline, decision process, current vendors
- Avoid: pitching the agency, quoting prices, promising deliverables

**Post-call** (within 24 hours):
- Send a written summary of what we heard, what we'd suggest as next step
- Update CRM with full notes
- Decide: scope a proposal, offer a paid Discovery, or decline

---

### W1.3 — Scoping Session
**Frequency**: Per qualified opportunity  
**Owner**: Engagement Partner + (if technical) Engineering Lead

**Format**: 2–3 hour working session, ideally on-site

**Agenda**:
1. Confirm understanding of business context (30 min)
2. Identify candidate use cases or scope (60 min)
3. Walk through our methodology and how it applies (30 min)
4. Discuss team, timeline, ballpark investment (30 min)
5. Define what would be in the proposal (15 min)

**Output**: Scoping notes that become the basis for the proposal

**Rule**: Never quote a firm price in a scoping session. Always come back with a written proposal.

---

### W1.4 — Proposal Development
**Frequency**: Per qualified opportunity post-scoping  
**Owner**: Engagement Partner (lead) + Engagement Lead (drafting support)

**Timeline**: Aim for 5–10 business days from scoping to proposal delivery

**Process**:
1. Draft using [proposal template](../02-commercial/proposal-template.md)
2. Internal partner review (24 hours turnaround)
3. Finalize, format, generate PDF
4. Send via email + courtesy follow-up call to walk through highlights

**Output**: PDF proposal, sent and acknowledged

---

### W1.5 — Pursuit and Close
**Frequency**: Ongoing during sales cycle  
**Owner**: Engagement Partner

**Recurring touchpoints**:
- Within 48 hours of proposal: confirmation call, walk through Q&A
- Weekly check-in until decision (light-touch — don't be a pest)
- Decision date confirmation
- Negotiation (if any)
- Close → SOW signature

**Loss handling**:
- Always ask why
- Document in CRM and in monthly loss review
- Stay in touch quarterly — many losses become future wins

## Engagement workflows

### W2.1 — Engagement Kickoff
**Frequency**: Per engagement, within 5 business days of SOW signature  
**Owner**: Engagement Lead

**Internal kickoff** (90 min):
- Review SOW, deliverables, schedule
- Walk team through client context
- Assign owner per deliverable
- Set up engagement repo, project tooling, folder structure
- Schedule recurring meetings

**Client kickoff** (half-day):
- Introductions and team bios
- Walk-through of approach, schedule, deliverables
- Confirm logistics, communication channels, meeting cadence
- Identify any new constraints or context
- Establish steering committee meeting cadence

**Output**: Engagement is live, all parties aligned

---

### W2.2 — Weekly Engagement Cadence
**Frequency**: Weekly during active engagement  
**Owner**: Engagement Lead

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Monday | Internal team standup (30 min) — what's planned this week, blockers |
| Tuesday/Wednesday | Client working sessions / interviews / development |
| Thursday | Weekly client status meeting (30–60 min) — progress, decisions needed, risks |
| Friday | Internal team retro (15 min) + status report draft |

**Weekly status report** to Engagement Partner: progress vs. plan, RAG status across [6 dimensions](../01-methodology/frameworks.md#8--engagement-health-dashboard-used-internally-every-engagement), upcoming risks. Always sent before end of business Friday.

---

### W2.3 — Engagement Health Review
**Frequency**: Weekly (internal) + bi-weekly (with Engagement Partner)  
**Owner**: Engagement Lead → Engagement Partner

Score across 6 dimensions per [framework](../01-methodology/frameworks.md#8--engagement-health-dashboard-used-internally-every-engagement). Any yellow or red triggers an Engagement Partner review and corrective action plan.

---

### W2.4 — Phase Gate Review
**Frequency**: At end of each phase  
**Owner**: Engagement Lead → Engagement Partner

**Internal review** (1 hour) before client-facing phase exit:
- Are all phase deliverables complete and quality-bar met?
- Are decisions documented?
- Are next-phase risks identified?
- Is the team ready for the next phase?

**Client phase gate** (per phase exit gate definition in [phases](../01-methodology/phases.md))

---

### W2.5 — Engagement Closeout
**Frequency**: Per engagement at conclusion  
**Owner**: Engagement Lead

**Activities**:
1. Final deliverable acceptance and sign-off
2. Final invoice
3. Handover document signed
4. Post-engagement client survey
5. Reference rights / case study confirmation
6. Internal post-mortem (see W4.1)
7. Archive engagement repo and all materials per retention policy
8. Transition to managed services contact (if applicable)

## Operational workflows

### W3.1 — Weekly Partner Sync
**Frequency**: Weekly, 60 min  
**Participants**: All partners  
**Owner**: Founding Partner

**Standing agenda**:
1. Pipeline review (10 min)
2. Active engagement health (15 min)
3. Personnel topics (10 min)
4. Strategic decisions on the table (15 min)
5. Open issues (10 min)

**Output**: Decisions logged in partner decisions doc

---

### W3.2 — Monthly Operating Review
**Frequency**: Monthly, 2 hours  
**Participants**: All partners + ops manager  
**Owner**: Founding Partner

**Standing agenda**:
1. Financials (revenue, gross margin, cash runway, A/R aging)
2. Pipeline (qualified pipeline by stage, win rates, average deal size)
3. Utilization (% billable across team, vs. targets)
4. Engagement health summary (any yellows / reds across portfolio)
5. People (open roles, attrition risks, performance issues)
6. Strategy / IP (methodology updates, content shipped, talks delivered)
7. Action items review

---

### W3.3 — Quarterly Business Review (Internal)
**Frequency**: Quarterly, half-day  
**Participants**: All partners + key leads  
**Owner**: Founding Partner

**Agenda**:
1. Quarter financial review
2. Pipeline health and forecast
3. Service line performance (which services are winning)
4. Methodology improvements shipped
5. Goals for next quarter
6. Any structural changes (org, comp, tooling)

**Output**: Quarterly priorities one-pager shared with the whole team

---

### W3.4 — Annual Strategic Planning
**Frequency**: Annual, two days off-site  
**Participants**: All partners  
**Owner**: Founding Partner

**Outputs**:
- Annual financial plan
- Hiring plan
- Service offering changes
- Brand / marketing investments
- Major bets

## Quality and learning workflows

### W4.1 — Engagement Post-Mortem
**Frequency**: Per engagement, within 2 weeks of close  
**Owner**: Engagement Lead

**Format**: 90-min retro session with the engagement team

**Questions**:
- What worked well? (specific examples)
- What didn't work? (specific examples — blameless)
- What surprised us? (positive or negative)
- What would we do differently?
- What's a candidate methodology improvement?
- What's a candidate playbook addition?

**Output**: Post-mortem doc filed in firm knowledge base; methodology change proposals routed to monthly Methodology Council (W4.4)

---

### W4.2 — Loss Review
**Frequency**: Monthly, 30 min  
**Owner**: Founding Partner

Review every loss in the past month:
- Why did we lose? (per documented reason from W1.5)
- Was the deal qualified properly?
- Was the proposal addressing the right things?
- Is there a pattern across losses?

**Output**: Adjustments to qualification, proposal template, or pricing as needed

---

### W4.3 — Quality Review
**Frequency**: Random sampling, 1–2 deliverables per week  
**Owner**: Rotating partner

A partner who was not on the engagement reviews a recent deliverable against the [universal quality bar](../03-deliverables/README.md#deliverable-quality-bar-universal). Surface gaps, file feedback to the engagement lead, and aggregate patterns into the Methodology Council.

---

### W4.4 — Methodology Council
**Frequency**: Monthly, 90 min  
**Participants**: All partners + senior leads  
**Owner**: Founding Partner (or rotating)

Review proposed methodology changes from post-mortems (W4.1) and quality reviews (W4.3). Decide which to incorporate into the official methodology. Owner of approved changes updates this repo.

**Output**: Changelog of methodology updates per quarter; commits to this repo with rationale.

## Hiring workflow

### W5.1 — Open Role
1. Hiring manager + Founding Partner align on role spec
2. Spec posted on careers page + targeted outreach
3. All candidates routed to one inbox to avoid duplicate work

### W5.2 — Screening
- Initial screen (30 min) by hiring manager
- Filter: clear fit, clear no, or maybe-with-task

### W5.3 — Working Interview
- Paid or scoped take-home task aligned to the actual role (real artifact, anonymized example)
- Candidate produces the artifact within 1 week
- Internal panel review of artifact

### W5.4 — Panel Round
- 2–3 interviews with future colleagues
- One interview specifically on values alignment / failure modes
- One interview specifically on technical/role-specific depth
- All interviewers submit independent written feedback before debrief

### W5.5 — Decision and Offer
- Hiring manager + Founding Partner align on decision
- Reference checks (3 minimum) for finalists
- Offer letter generated by ops within 48 hours of decision

### W5.6 — Onboarding
- 30-60-90 plan written before start date
- Buddy assigned (a peer, not the manager)
- First-month check-ins weekly with manager
- 90-day formal review
