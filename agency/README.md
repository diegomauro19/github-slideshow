# AI Consulting Agency — Foundational Playbook

This is the operating playbook for a full-stack AI consulting agency targeting enterprise clients. It's structured so anyone joining the firm — partner, consultant, engineer, ops — can read the relevant section and understand how we work.

## Who this is for

- **Partners and founders** — the source of truth for our positioning, pricing, and operating model
- **Engagement leads** — the playbook for running engagements end-to-end
- **Sales** — the templates and pricing rules to quote and close
- **New hires** — the canonical "how things work here" document

## What this is NOT

- A business plan (financial projections, fundraising, etc. live elsewhere)
- A brand book (visual identity, messaging, voice live in the marketing repo)
- Compensation structure (sensitive — lives in a private internal doc)
- Specific client work (lives in per-engagement repos)

## The agency in one page

**What we do**: Full-stack enterprise AI consulting — strategy, build, and managed services. Vendor-neutral. Senior-only delivery.

**Who we serve**: Fortune 500 / large enterprise clients running material AI programs. Engagement sizes start at $75k (audits) and go to $5M+ (multi-year programs). Sweet spot: $250k–$1M per engagement.

**How we deliver**: Five-phase methodology — Discover, Assess, Design, Build, Scale — with fixed deliverables and decision gates at each phase. Every engagement is structured to cross the POC-to-production gap that kills most enterprise AI work.

**What makes us different**:
1. **Senior-only delivery.** We staff partner, principal, and senior consultant — never associates "learning on the client's dime."
2. **Vendor-neutral.** No reseller margin, no platform commissions. Our model selection is documented with rationale.
3. **Production-shaped from day one.** Every artifact, even in Discover, supports the eventual production system.
4. **Eval-first.** Nothing ships without an eval set. AI systems behave like software, not magic.
5. **Hand off, don't hand over.** The client team can run, modify, and extend everything we build.

**How we make money**: Fixed-fee for strategy, fixed-fee with change control for builds, monthly retainer + metered usage for managed services, optional outcome-based upside layered on top.

**What we don't do**: Free POCs to win deals. Deck-and-pray strategy work. Bottom-of-market price competition. Reselling vendor platforms.

## How this playbook stays alive

This is a living document. Three feedback loops keep it sharp:

1. **Engagement post-mortems** (W4.1 in workflows) surface what worked and what didn't. Methodology changes go to the Methodology Council.
2. **Quality reviews** (W4.3) catch deliverables drifting from the playbook.
3. **Methodology Council** (W4.4) approves changes monthly. Approved changes become commits to this repo with rationale in the commit message.

If you read something here and it doesn't match how things are actually done, that's a bug. File a methodology change proposal.
